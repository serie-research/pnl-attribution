<WhatToNotice>

With three signals, the empty coalition and the coalition of the other two
signals each get Shapley weight one third.
Each one-signal coalition gets weight one sixth.

The total interaction is the full value minus the sum of stand-alone values.
Unlike the proxy residual, it involves no fit. On a desk, the game exists only
once each coalition's portfolio and risk convention are specified.
