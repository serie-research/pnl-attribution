<WhatToNotice>

Inside the band, both attributed trades are zero, even when one signal alone
would trade. Outside it, Durin turns the spread into an extra cost that both
signals share. Their attributed trades add up to the actual trade, even if one
goes in the opposite direction. The figure uses Durin's static model
[@durinSignalwisePerformanceAttribution2014, p. 17, Eqs. 49–54].

</WhatToNotice>
