<WhatToNotice>

At the default zero factor exposure, specific P&L equals total P&L. Increase exposure to 4: factor shocks then cause most of the day-to-day variance, while the model's expected gain still comes only from the specific signal. Select a short history, then press “New history” to see how realised mean shares change. The sample covariance between factor and specific P&L can be nonzero even though the model sets it to zero.
